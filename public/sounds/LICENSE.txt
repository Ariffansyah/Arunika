Sounds

Footsteps, glass, wood, rock and landing impacts: "Impact Sounds" by Kenney (kenney.nl).
Doors: "RPG Audio" by Kenney. Click, tick, switch, correct and wrong: "Interface Sounds" by Kenney.
Converted to mono MP3 so every browser can play them.

The siren, rumble, kentongan, splashes, rain, wind, waves, city hum, flood water and birdsong are
synthesised in the browser (components/campus/sound.ts), so there is no file for them.
