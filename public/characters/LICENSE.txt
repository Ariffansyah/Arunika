Characters

Bodies, clothes, heads and the idle, walk, run and wave animations:
"Ultimate Modular Men Pack" and "Ultimate Modular Women Pack" by Quaternius (quaternius.com),
via poly.pizza. Parts are mixed and recoloured into an SMP school uniform.

Crouch, sit, the drop-cover-hold-on pose, the ducked walk and the jump are transferred from
"Universal Animation Library" by Quaternius (quaternius.com).
